Sift – Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026 · Applies to the Sift browser extension

In short: Sift does not collect, send, sell or share any of your data. Everything you save stays on your own computer, inside your browser. There are no accounts, no tracking and no analytics.

What the extension does

When you click the Sift icon on a page, the extension reads the recipe on that page (ingredients, method, title, image link and similar details) so it can show you a clean version, scale the servings and convert units. If you choose to save a recipe, it is stored in your Recipe Book.

Data stored on your device

If you save a recipe, the following is stored locally in your browser using the browser's own extension storage (chrome.storage.local):

  • The recipe details read from the page, including the page address.
  • Anything you add yourself: notes, star rating, tags, line edits, and your chosen servings and unit settings.
  • The date and version number from when the extension was first installed on your computer. It never leaves your device.

This data never leaves your device through Sift. The developer has no access to it and cannot see it.

Data we do not collect

  • No personal information, such as your name, email address or location.
  • No browsing history. The extension does nothing on a page until you click its icon, and it only looks at that one page.
  • No analytics, usage statistics, cookies, advertising identifiers or crash reports.
  • No data is sold, rented, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or credit decisions.

Requests made by your browser

The extension does not contact any server operated by the developer. The only network requests are the ones your browser makes in the normal way when it shows a recipe's picture: the image is loaded from the website that hosts it, as it already is on the original page. We ask the browser not to send a referrer with those requests. Links such as "original page" simply open the recipe's own website.

Why each permission is needed

  • activeTab and scripting: to read the recipe on the page you are looking at, only after you click the icon or use the keyboard shortcut.
  • storage: to keep your saved recipes and settings on your device.
  • contextMenus: to add an "Open Recipe Book" item to the toolbar icon's right-click menu.

The extension does not request access to all websites and does not load or run code from the internet.

Export, import and deletion

  • You can export your Recipe Book to a file and import it again from the Recipe Book page. That file is created and saved by you, on your device.
  • You can delete any recipe from the Recipe Book at any time.
  • Removing the extension from your browser deletes all of its stored data. Clearing the browser's extension data does the same.

Children

Sift is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. Because it collects no data, it collects no data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the extension's data practices change, for example if optional paid features that need an online check are added, this policy will be updated before the change is released, and the effective date above will change.
